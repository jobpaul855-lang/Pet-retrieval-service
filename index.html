How we can help

Every situation is different. We discuss the circumstances, available methods, permissions, and practical limitations before a search begins.

🚁

Thermal-drone-assisted searching

Where conditions, regulations, weather, terrain, and permissions allow, thermal imaging may help scan suitable areas. It cannot guarantee that a pet will be detected.

🐾

Missing-pet search support

Compassionate communication
